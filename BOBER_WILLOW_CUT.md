## The farm

You play Bober, yellow hat, on foot. Drag looks. Tap walks. Nib, Puddle, and Twig live in the lodge. Each morning you can hand each of them one chore: field, barn, or mill. They walk to it on the map and do it. You can do any chore yourself. There is no survival job row and no heat bar.

The map is one canvas, about six phone screens wide and five tall. Places:

- The acre: lodge door, field strips, barn, pond
- Moss's creek market, about 15 seconds from the door
- The willow lease, about 15 seconds the other way

No human town. No tractors. No 3D. The far bank is visible and closed. Winter snows this same map. It does not load the old fire campaign.

A day runs while you walk and caps near 3 minutes if you never sleep. Sleep ends the day whenever you choose. Crops advance at dawn. Nothing grows on a real-time phone timer. There is no energy bar, no ads, no wallet.

## Simulator systems, version 1

Soil on every bed, and it lasts: wild, stony, tilled, watered, seeded, growing, ready, stubble. Fertility falls at harvest and comes back from chaff or bedding. Skip water and that bed waits. A crop in the wrong season withers at the season change.

Four seasons, 7 days each, then year 2 on the same land.

Crops:

- Reedbean, spring, ready in 3 dawns
- Pond beet, summer, ready in 5 dawns
- Lanternpod, fall, ready in 4 dawns
- Willow stool on the lease, spring through fall, cut and it regrows in 4 dawns. This is the crop the title names. Winter it rests.

Animals, and they do not die:

- Reedswans on the pond. An egg at dawn only if the trough had fodder.
- Plodders in the barn. Plod cream only if they were fed. Later they pull a sled. Not in the first mornings.

The mill is a building, not a hut upgrade. One reedbean sack becomes meal only while the mill is stocked. Meal sells for more than the sack. The mill also leaves chaff, which is how fertility returns. A mill that is empty does nothing.

Money is $bober. Moss's stall posts a price per season. You sell there, and you buy seed there. Storing a crop is allowed. Selling is not automatic.

## First ten minutes

Spring, day 1, daylight, no snow. Bober stands at the lodge door. Two reedbean shoots are already up. Two beds are tilled and empty. The barn is cut off at the edge of the screen. One reedswan is on the pond. A path leaves the frame. The corner reads SPRING · DAY 1.

You water the shoots or hand Puddle the field. You cut grass into the trough or hand Nib the barn. You walk to the willow stools and see they are not ready to cut. You walk to Moss, see the spring prices, and do not have a full sack yet. You sleep. Day 2 tells you the reedbean stage and whether the swan left an egg. If the trough was empty, there is no egg.
